    GPIO ORGANIZATION IN STM32L4SXXX
•   GPIO: General Purpose Input-Output
•   STM32L4Sxxx have up to 140 I/O lines, organized into ports and pins
•   GPIO ports:
     •   GPIOA to GPIOI (up to 16 I/O pins per port)
     •   Exact number of pins implemented depends on the specific SoC (System-on-chip)




•   For STM32L45SVI:
     •   83 GPIOs
     •   Port A to Port E -> 16 pins each

     •   Port H -> 3 pins

    GPIO ORGANIZATION IN STM32L4SXXX MCU
•   Direct connection to AHB2 bus (120 MHz) allows fast I/O timing
•   GPIO peripheral is used to interface with
     •   Inputs like Switches
     •   Outputs like LEDs
     •   Embedded/on-chip peripherals for both digital & analog signals

    GPIO MULTIPLEXING IN STM32
•    Each pin of the GPIO ports can be individually configured by software in
     several modes & directions:
     •   Input
     •   Output
     •   Analog
     •   Alternative function (AF)
•    The number of physical pins are kept low by multiplexing different functions
     on a particular pin – helps to keep the cost low
•    Possible mutual exclusivity of functions, but typical applications need only a
     small subset of the various peripheral interfaces available on an SoC
•    GPIO lines are configurable through GPIO registers, which have to be
     accessed as 32-bit words, half-words or bytes                                                                               6
